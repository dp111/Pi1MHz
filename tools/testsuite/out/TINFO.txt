9001 REM Resident integers survive CHAIN: Z%=1 chain all tests, Y%=index,
9002 REM V%/W%/U% = running PASS/FAIL/SKIP totals.  Lines the host parses:
9003 REM   B:<test>  T:<test>:<check>:PASS|FAIL|SKIP:<why>  I:<test>:<name>:<text>
9004 REM   S:<test>:P=n:F=n:K=n   then ##DONE (host mode) or ##ALLDONE (chain mode)

9010 DEF PROCchk(N$,O%):IF O% p%=p%+1:V%=V%+1:PRINT "T:";T$;":";N$;":PASS" ELSE f%=f%+1:W%=W%+1:PRINT "T:";T$;":";N$;":FAIL"
9015 ENDPROC
9020 DEF PROCskip(N$,R$):k%=k%+1:U%=U%+1:PRINT "T:";T$;":";N$;":SKIP:";R$:ENDPROC
